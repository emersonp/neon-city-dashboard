Boons and Drawbacks

1-2 Boons (each)

  • Attack that hit and enemy: +10 damage
  • Progressing an objective: +10 impact (even if failure)
  • Attack on a minion squad (that hit): squad size −1
  • Restore 10 luck

3-4 Boons

  • Give the player a deep secret about a person, place, or event in the scene
  • Give another player in the scene a positive condition, with the condition’s severity equal to the number of boons rolled
  • Give an enemy in the scene a negative condition, with the condition’s severity equal to the number of boons rolled
  • Give the player 1 influence due to an unexpected windfall
  • Allow the player to mark any 1 skill as having been successfully used this session(but not a skill used to make the roll that generated the boon)

1-2 Drawbacks (each)

  • Add 1 heat to the heat pool
  • Lose 10 luck
  • Attack that hit an enemy: −10 damage
  • Gear used gets the Damaged condition (or worsens it)

3-4 Drawbacks

  • The character gets a negative condition (severity = drawbacks rolled)
  • Add a negative condition with severity ×2 to the scene
  • Add a new urgent objective to the scene, difficulty 50
  • Immediately introduce a minor complication to the scene without spending heat
  • For each drawback spent, a major villain in the scene regains 10 luck
  • Destroy or negate an asset that is present in the scene and that the players either possess or are attempting to acquire
