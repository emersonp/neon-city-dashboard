    Boons and Drawbacks

    1-2 Boons (each)

    • Attack (that hit) an enemy: +10 damage
    • Progressing an objective: +10 impact (even if failure)
    • Attack (that hit) a minion squad: squad size −1
    • Restore 10 luck

    3-4 Boons

    • Give the player a deep secret about a person, place, or event in the scene
    • Give another player in the scene a positive condition, with the condition’s severity equal to the number of boons rolled
    • Give an enemy in the scene a negative condition, with the condition’s severity equal to the number of boons rolled
    • Give the player 1 influence due to an unexpected windfall
    • Allow the player to mark any 1 skill as having been successfully used this session(but not a skill used to make the roll that generated the boon)

    1-2 Drawbacks (each)

    • Add 1 heat to the heat pool
    • Lose 10 luck
    • Attack (that hit) an enemy: −10 damage
    • Gear used gets the Damaged condition (or worsens it)

    3-4 Drawbacks

    • The character gets a negative condition (severity = drawbacks rolled)
    • Add a negative condition with severity ×2 to the scene
    • Add a new urgent objective to the scene, difficulty 50
    • Immediately introduce a minor complication to the scene without spending heat
    • For each drawback spent, a major villain in the scene regains 10 luck
    • Destroy or negate an asset that is present in the scene and that the players either possess or are attempting to acquire

    Montage actions

    • Blow off Steam: Recover all luck by de-stressing in a manner the player describes
    • Coordinate with Allies: When a player chooses this action, the Administrator sets a 3-minute timer and allows players to freely discuss their next steps just as they would in a planning scene. No more than 2 crew members can choose this action in a single day.
    • Cultivate Contacts: If a character has 0 Influence, they can gain 1 influence from the supply
    • Go Shopping: Acquire 1 item of common gear
    • Load: The character loads into a shell they have access to, overlaying the appropriate shell sheet
    • Recover: Heal 1 wound by receiving medical care or repairs
    • Prepare: Perform a time-consuming but risk-free task in preparation for an upcoming scene
    • Research: Learn publicly available information
    • Use a Perk: Use an available archetype perk or body augmentation that requires a montage action

    Spending influence

    • To use a character perk that specifically requires influence for activation
    • To take a single, extra montage action during legwork
    • To allow an ally in the same scene to reroll all the dice in a roll they just made
    • To spend 1 favor owed to the character’s faction in order to get something the character needs
    • When the Administrator tells them to; usually, this happens when a character tries to accomplish a task that requires more effort than their personal actions could account for.

    Pushing your luck

    The character spends luck to increase their chance of success to the value that would make the player’s roll successful. Pushing your luck spends luck in increments of 10.

    A character can push their luck only when making a check and never when making an attack, unless the character has a perk or augmentation that says otherwise.

    For every 10 luck a character spends when pushing their luck, the player must also roll one challenge die (), adding it to the initial check. If pushing your luck would require a character to spend so much luck that they would have to add more than 4 challenge dice to a roll (including any challenge dice already assigned to the roll), then the character cannot push their luck.

    *This is one possible outcome of rolling a drawback.

    There can never be more than 30 heat in the heat pool.

    Creating Legwork Scenes

    Describe one thing you want to get out of the scene and where the scene takes place in the city, including the district and a specific location therein. Then describe how you are going to get what you want and, if necessary, who you interact with to get it.

    The Administrator then describes any obstacles inherent in the scene and adds 2 heat () to the heat pool. Then, other players can decide to participate in the scene, and as a group the players explain how the crew deals with each obstacle.
