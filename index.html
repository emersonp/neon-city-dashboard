Boons and Drawbacks


            
1–2 Boons (each)

            
    
              
  • Attack that hit: +10 damage
    • 
              
  • Progressing an objective: +10 impact
    • 
              
  • Attack on a minion squad: squad size −1
    • 
              
  • Restore 10 luck
    • 
            


            
3–4 Boons

            
    
              
  • Player learns a deep secret about a person, place, or event in the scene
    • 
              
  • Another player gets a positive condition (severity = boons rolled)
    • 
              
  • An enemy in the scene gets a negative condition (severity = boons rolled)
    • 
              
  • Player gains 1 influence
    • 
              
  • Mark any 1 skill as used this session (not the one used for this roll)
    • 
            


            
1–2 Drawbacks (each)

            
    
              
  • +1 heat  to the heat pool
    • 
              
  • Lose 10 luck
    • 
              
  • Attack that hit: −10 damage
    • 
              
  • Gear used gets the Damaged condition (or worsens it)
    • 
            


            
3–4 Drawbacks

            
    
              
  • Character gets a negative condition (severity = drawbacks rolled)
    • 
              
  • Scene gets a negative condition (severity ×2)
    • 
              
  • New urgent objective, difficulty 50
    • 
              
  • Minor complication enters the scene, no heat spent
    • 
              
  • Each drawback: a major villain in the scene regains 10 luck
    • 
              
  • Destroy or negate an asset the players have or want
